What if your glasses could read your dog? 🐕
⠀
I built DogSense HUD, a Symbolic Synapse prototype for the Rokid RV101 smart glasses. It watches a dog's body language, listens to its barks, and shows what it measures right on the lens.
⠀
◈ 24 tracking points on the dog's body
◈ Pitch and rhythm of every bark
◈ My own voice filtered out. Yes, it once thought my "hey, hey, hey!" was barking 😅
◈ Clear rules, no black box. Every reading shows why
⠀
Tested on my own two dogs, in the garden and through the gate.
⠀
Measured, never imagined.
