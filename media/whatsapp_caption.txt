*What if your glasses could read your dog?* 🐕
⠀
Meet *DogSense HUD*, my new Symbolic Synapse prototype for the Rokid RV101 glasses. It watches a dog's posture and listens to its barks, then shows what it picks up on the glasses' display.
⠀
◈ Tracks 24 points on the dog's body
◈ Reads pitch and rhythm in the barks (and ignores my voice)
◈ Explains every reading. No black box
⠀
Tested on my own dogs, including through the gate 😄
⠀
_Measured, never imagined._
⠀
